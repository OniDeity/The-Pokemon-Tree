# The Pokémon Tree: project memory

A Pokémon incremental game on The Modding Tree 2.6.6.2 (plain JS, Vue 2 from a CDN, no build
step). It's Idle Pokémon's original plan done in TMT rather than Profectus: Professor Oak's field
researcher searches routes, battles with the party, catches Pokémon, and resets for Gym badges.
Sister project: OniDeity/idle-pokemon (its data is the source of ours).

## Workflow

- Develop on the session's feature branch, open a PR into `dev`, merge when the checks are green.
  `dev` deploys to https://onideity.github.io/The-Pokemon-Tree/dev/ (its own save id, via
  `scripts/prepareDevBuild.mjs`).
- `dev` exists so a save-breaking bug never reaches the public game. Before releasing, start a
  game on the `main` code, then load that save in the new code (same origin) and check it loads
  with no page errors and keeps its progress. If it does, release without waiting to be asked:
  PR `dev` → `main`, merge when green, confirm https://onideity.github.io/The-Pokemon-Tree/ serves
  the new code.
- Deploys go to the `gh-pages` branch (`.github/workflows/deploy.yml`); Pages serves that branch.
  The deploy stamps each build (`scripts/stampBuild.mjs`): script and stylesheet addresses get
  `?v=<commit>`, and `index.html` checks `version.txt` (never cached) and reloads once if a newer
  build is live, because Pages lets browsers cache files for 10 minutes.
- Never put model identifiers in commits, PRs or code.
- Check changes in a browser: serve the folder (`python3 -m http.server`) and open `index.html`.
  Headless Chromium needs the agent proxy bypassed for localhost.
- Checks (CI, `.github/workflows/test.yml`): `node --check` on every file in `js/`, and a two-hour
  simulation per starter (`SIM_HOURS=2`) that fails if it can't earn a badge.
- Pacing: `node scripts/simulate.js <starter> <seed>` plays greedily and prints each badge's time
  (and whether the survey or the team was the wall). Check all three starters and two seeds.

## Layout

- `js/data/kanto.js`, `js/data/johto.js` and `js/data/hoenn.js`: generated by `scripts/buildData.py` from Idle
  Pokémon's species (#1-386; friendship evolutions level up, held-item trades are trades with an
  `item`), encounter, trainer and special-encounter data (Kanto: FRLG tables, #1-151 only; Johto:
  HGSS, walk/surf/rods, time-of-day weights averaged). johto.json is dumped from Idle Pokémon's
  johto.ts with tsx (JOHTO_ZONES, JOHTO_GYMS, johtoFinale(), JOHTO_SPECIALS); hoenn.json likewise
  from hoenn.ts (Emerald; walk/surf/rods/rockSmash/dive from encounters.json; Feebas's extra tiles
  aren't in yet). Dump scripts live in the scratchpad's `dump/` (re-create them if gone).
- `js/engine.js`: pure rules (stats, deterministic battles, catching, experience, level caps,
  Gym/League strengths). `js/logic.js`: rules over `player` (multipliers, the Field's equipment,
  zones and surveys, encounters, box/party/evolution, trainers, specials). Neither uses TMT
  helpers, so the simulator runs them against a fake `player`.
- Layers (`js/layers/`): `f` Field (row 0, main; Research Points are TMT's `player.points`),
  `t` Team (row 0, kept through badges, reset by the Hall of Fame), `g` Gyms (row 1, custom prestige: one badge per
  reset, needs the survey done and a winning battle forecast), `l` League (row 2), `h` Hall of
  Fame (row 3, custom prestige for Fame; resets everything but the Pokédex, achievements and
  itself; holds the Fame shop and TMT challenges), `c` Day Care (row 1 beside the Gyms; kept
  through badges, reset by the Hall of Fame; rules `dayCareTick`/`eggStep`/`eggSpecies`, breeding
  needs `SPECIES[id].gender`), `s` Safari Zone (row 1; trips of Safari Balls, no battles,
  `safariTick`; same reset rules as the Day Care), `k` Game Corner (row 1; coins are its points,
  bought with RP (`COIN_RP`) or won at the slots (`SLOT_RESULTS`, ~0.9 back per coin bet until the
  Lucky Machine); prize Pokémon (`PRIZE_COINS`) cost coins, not RP; jackpots in `player.d.jackpots`
  count for good), `y` Shiny hunting (row 2; a target species' chain multiplies its shiny odds,
  `chainBonus`/`huntStep`, the Lure through `rollEncounter`'s `lure`; shiny species in
  `player.d.shiny` give +1% RP each and `SHINY_MILESTONES`), `q` Story (row 2, `layers/story.js`,
  v0.18; replaced Team Rocket `r` and Trainers `v`, which `migrateStory` folds in): the story as
  `STORY_CHAPTERS` in logic.js, places whose trainers are fought in order
  (`player.q.progress[chapter]`, kept through badges), each with an `arc` (rival, villain,
  challenger: the tabs; rival-only chapters default to rival). The Storyline tab draws the
  region's chapters as a map by the badges they need (`storyMapHtml`, `chapterOpens`). A cleared
  chapter can gate a Gym (`gate`, the badge index) or the League (`gateLeague`), `needs` takes a
  chapter or a list, and `SPECIAL_STORY` gates gifts. The rival's teams follow the starter
  (`rivalTeam`). Rematch calls (`storyCallStep` per wild battle, adding its search time; every
  `CALL_SECONDS` a cleared chapter of the region calls, up to `maxCalls()`): the last trainer at
  the party's cap and 0.75× the last Gym beaten, paying a third of a story battle's RP; they
  count only as story wins (`player.d.storyWins`), not Rockets/rivals: counting those too made
  first runs 35% faster. Lifetime `ROCKET_`/`RIVAL_`/`STORY_MILESTONES` run into the hundreds.
  The simulator plays `storyTick` and `rematchTick`. Side layers `d` Pokédex (with the Diploma, `hasDiploma`) and `a` Achievements.
- Popups go through `pkPopup(kind, ...)` / `pkLog`, and `notifyOn(kind)` reads the player's
  switches (`NOTIFY_KINDS`, kept in TMT's `options.pkNotify`; the settings page draws
  `pk-notify-settings`, and `game.js`'s `shouldNotify` checks `glow`). Long lists of Pokémon get a
  `["pk-search", field]` box and `filterBar(layer, field, modes)`; their fields live in the layer
  and survive its reset.

## Gotchas

- TMT calls every function on a layer each tick to fill `tmp`: keep helpers with side effects
  outside `addLayer`.
- `js/technical/loader.js` sets `script.async = false` so `modFiles` run in order (TMT's
  `setAttribute("async", "false")` still loaded them async). `index.html` needs `<meta charset>`
  or "Pokédex" breaks the scripts.
- Trainer strength multiplies both attack and bulk, so it acts roughly squared.
- TMT's `layerDataReset` merges start data into objects and arrays instead of replacing them;
  reset layers with `freshLayerData` (field.js) or the box, routes and gifts survive the reset.
- Automation challenges (31-33) unlock `AUTOMATIONS` (toggles in `player.h.auto`). Auto-buy
  (`autoBuyTick`) also buys the one-time upgrades (Field, Day Care, Safari, hunting; the Game
  Corner's with coins). Box Training (`train`, challenge 42 like Team Coach; `trainingParty`) keeps the
  fewest of `bestParty` that still win `nextOpponent` and puts `trainees` (level evolutions ahead)
  in the back slots for the Exp. Share; the simulator doesn't use it. The Field's `doReset` keeps `searching` on when the Drone is kept. Hatchling Run
  and Poacher's Net weaken Pokémon except hatched/Safari ones (`challengeHandicap`, box entries'
  `origin`); the simulator can't breed or go on Safari, so it only shows their worst case.
- Challenges must stay beatable by the simulator: Battle Gear is most of a team's power, so no
  challenge takes it away, and Lone Partner doubles its one Pokémon (`LONE_PARTNER_BOOST`).
- Box and party entries are keyed by species and look: `boxKey(id, look)` gives "15", "15-shiny",
  "150-clone" or "25-clone-shiny" (`makeLook`, `speciesOf`, `formOf`, `isShiny`, `keyName`).
  Each look is its own slot, so evolution only waits when the same look of the evolution is
  owned; forms (`FORMS` in engine.js) never evolve. `migrateBox` (run by `fixOldSave`) moved
  v0.2 saves (one entry per species with a `shiny` flag, numeric party) to these keys.
- Encounters per tick are capped (`MAX_ENCOUNTERS_PER_TICK`); the rest waits in `player.f.progress`
  so offline time isn't lost.

## Plans (the owner's direction)

- Each region's first run should take about a day of play, reached mostly by going wide with
  features (new nodes, small bonuses that add up across resets) rather than slower numbers.
- Done: Hall of Fame prestige and challenges (v0.2); Kanto's anime places and forms (v0.4):
  `buildData.py` turns Idle Pokémon's anime zones into zones with `anime` and `hof` (the Hall of
  Fame entries they need, by badge tier), and its one-of-a-kind ids into forms. Gen 2 species
  seen there wait for Johto. Pinkan and Valencian forms belong to the Orange Islands.
- Next: side systems that each add a node and a bonus, in the owner's order: Day Care (done,
  v0.5), Safari Zone (done, v0.6), automation challenges (v0.7), Game Corner (done, v0.8), shiny
  hunting (done, v0.9), Rocket Hideout (v0.10), Kanto's story (v0.11: Rockets, rival, S.S. Anne, Dojo, gates, Diploma,
  Missingno., box pages, hidden sprites). The simulator buys coins for prizes but doesn't play the slots.
- Later regions follow the same pattern, gated by Hall of Fame entries (Johto: 3 Kanto entries;
  more for Hoenn).

## Johto plan (agreed with the owner, not built yet)

The owner wants every Hall of Fame to feel like a proper reset:
- (Done in v0.12.) The Pokédex (seen, caught) and the Breeder's notes (hatched) reset at every Hall of Fame entry;
  Pokédex milestones become per-run goals and the Diploma must be earned within one run (its
