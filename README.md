# The Pokémon Tree

An incremental Pokémon game on [The Modding Tree](https://github.com/Acamaeda/The-Modding-Tree)
(TMT 2.6.6.2). It follows Idle Pokémon's original plan: you're Professor Oak's field researcher.

- **Field (F):** search Kanto's routes (FireRed/LeafGreen encounter tables) for wild Pokémon.
  Your party battles each one; beat it and you earn Research Points and a chance to catch it.
  Research Points buy field equipment and open the next routes of the area.
- **Team (T):** your starter, party and box. Pokémon level up to the level cap and evolve.
  They're kept through every reset, as is the Pokédex.
- **Gyms (G):** survey the area (every route open, every species seen), then beat its Gym
  Leader. The badge is the prestige: it resets Research Points, equipment and routes, and opens
  the next area. Beating Brock is the first reset.
- **Pokémon League (L):** after eight badges, the Elite Four and the Champion.
- **Hall of Fame (H):** after becoming Champion, start the region over for Fame. Fame buys
  permanent upgrades, and challenges (Nuzlocke, Monotype, Lone Partner, Light Pack,
  Underleveled) replay the region under a rule for a lasting reward. The Pokédex is kept.
- **Pokédex (D)** and **Achievements (A)** on the side; Pokédex milestones boost everything.

Play it by opening `index.html` (or serving the folder with any static server).

## Code

- `js/data/kanto.js`: species, type chart, zones, Gyms, Elite Four, Champions and special
  encounters, generated by `scripts/buildData.py` from Idle Pokémon's data.
- `js/engine.js`: pure rules (stats, battles, catching, experience).
- `js/logic.js`: the game's rules over `player` (multipliers, encounters, the box, trainers).
- `js/layers/*.js`: the TMT layers. `js/mod.js` and `js/tree.js`: TMT's mod settings.
- `scripts/simulate.js`: plays the game headlessly with a greedy strategy and reports when each
  badge falls: `node scripts/simulate.js <starter 1|4|7> <seed>`.
- `docs/`: TMT's own documentation.
